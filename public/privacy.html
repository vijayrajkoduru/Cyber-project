Privacy Policy

VulnusLab  ·  Effective date: 1 July 2026  ·  Last updated: 1 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VulnusLab (“we”, “us”, the “Service”), operated by Vijay Kumar, collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use vulnuslab.com and app.vulnuslab.com. We comply with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”).

1. Who we are

The Service is operated by Vijay Kumar (the “Data Fiduciary”). For any privacy question or request, contact us at awsvijju5@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

  • Account data: your username, email address, and a password (stored only as a salted one-way hash — we never store your plaintext password).
  • Usage & scan data: the target addresses/URLs you submit for scanning, scan results and history, the plan you are on, and daily usage counts.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, and server logs, collected automatically to operate and secure the Service.
  • Billing data (paid plans only): if you purchase a plan, payment is processed by our third-party payment provider. We receive a transaction status and plan record; we do not store your full card details.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and operate your account and provide the scanning features you request.
  • To enforce plan limits, prevent abuse, and secure the Service (rate limiting, fraud/abuse detection).
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions (paid plans).
  • To communicate with you about your account, security, and service changes.
  • To meet legal obligations.

4. Authorized use of scanning features

VulnusLab is a security-testing tool. You must only scan systems, applications, or networks that you own or are explicitly authorized to test. You are solely responsible for the targets you submit. We log scan activity and may retain records to investigate misuse or comply with law.

5. How we share data

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Infrastructure providers that host the Service and store backups (hosting and offsite backup storage).
  • Payment provider (paid plans only) to process transactions.
  • Authorities where required by law or to protect rights, safety, and the Service.

6. Data retention

We keep your account and scan data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain limited records to comply with legal, security, or accounting obligations. Encrypted backups are rotated on a 14-day cycle.

7. Data security

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit over HTTPS/TLS.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes.
  • Access to administrative functions is authenticated and role-restricted.
  • Backups are stored offsite. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data.

8. Cookies & local storage

We use browser local storage to keep you signed in (a session token). We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

9. Your rights (DPDP Act)

Subject to the DPDP Act, you may:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we process about you.
  • Request correction, completion, updating, or erasure of your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent (this may prevent you from using parts of the Service).
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Raise a grievance (see below).

To exercise any right, email awsvijju5@gmail.com.

10. Grievance Officer

As required by the DPDP Act, you may contact our Grievance Officer for any concern about your personal data:

  • Name: Vijay Kumar
  • Email: awsvijju5@gmail.com

We will acknowledge and respond to grievances within the timelines required by applicable law.

11. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. International users

The Service is operated from India and your data is processed there. By using the Service you consent to this processing.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here and update the “Last updated” date. Material changes will be communicated where appropriate.
