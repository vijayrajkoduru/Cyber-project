2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide, operate, and improve the Service and generate your reports;
  • To process payments, send receipts, and manage your subscription;
  • To send transactional email (account, credentials, billing, security notices);
  • To protect the Service against abuse and unauthorized use;
  • To respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your scan targets or results for any purpose other than delivering the Service to you.

3. Legal Basis & Consent

We process your data on the basis of your consent (given when you create an account and submit scans) and to perform our contract with you. You may withdraw consent at any time by closing your account, subject to data we must retain for legal or accounting purposes.

4. Storage & Retention

Your data is stored on infrastructure we control. Each user's scan history, targets, and reports are isolated to that user's account. Scan records and reports are retained for a minimum of 90 days and then per your account status; you may request earlier deletion (see Your Rights). Billing records are retained as required by Indian tax and accounting law.

5. Third Parties We Share With

We share the minimum data necessary with service providers who help us operate:

  • Razorpay — payment processing (handles your card/bank data directly under their own privacy policy);
  • Email provider — to deliver transactional email (your email address and message content only);
  • Hosting / infrastructure providers — to run the platform.

These providers are bound to use your data only to provide their service to us.

6. Security

We apply technical and organizational safeguards including encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords, token-based authentication, per-user data isolation, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and to notify affected users of any breach as required by law.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law (including the DPDP Act), you may:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate data;
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data;
  • Withdraw consent and request that we stop processing;
  • Raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer.

To exercise any right, email support@vulnuslab.com. We respond within a reasonable period and as required by law.

8. Cookies

The dashboard uses essential browser storage (e.g. a session token) to keep you logged in and to operate the Service. We do not use third-party advertising trackers. The marketing site may use minimal analytics; no sale of data occurs.

9. Children

The Service is not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. International Transfers

Our infrastructure and providers may process data outside your country. Where this happens, we take steps to ensure your data remains protected consistent with this policy.

11. Grievance Officer / Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or grievances under the DPDP Act, contact our Grievance Officer at support@vulnuslab.com. Postal and full business details are on our Contact page.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
