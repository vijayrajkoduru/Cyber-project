Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 13 June 2026

VulnusLab (Udyam Registration UDYAM-AP-13-0090768, India) provides digital subscription services. Because access to scanners and reports is provisioned to your account immediately on payment, the following policy applies.

1. Digital Service — Access Is Immediate

VulnusLab is a digital service. The moment your payment succeeds, your plan, scan quota, and access are activated. Once you have initiated a scan or your paid access has been provisioned, the corresponding payment is considered consumed and is non-refundable, except as set out below.

2. Erroneous or Duplicate Charges

If you were charged in error, charged more than once for the same plan, or did not receive the access you paid for, contact us within 7 days of the charge and we will issue a full refund of the erroneous amount after verification.

3. Cancellation of a Subscription

  • You may cancel a recurring subscription at any time from your account or by emailing support.
  • Cancellation stops all future renewals.
  • Your access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • We do not provide pro-rated refunds for the unused portion of a current billing period.

4. How to Request a Refund

Email support@vulnuslab.com from the address on your account with your transaction ID / payment reference and the reason for the request. We aim to acknowledge within 24 hours.

5. Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method through our payment partner (Razorpay), typically within 5–7 business days. The time for the amount to appear in your account depends on your bank or card issuer.

6. Free Trials

Where a free trial is offered, no charge is made during the trial and therefore no refund applies to it. You will only be billed if you choose a paid plan.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@vulnuslab.com or see our Contact page.
